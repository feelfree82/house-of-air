## Real talk

People kept asking how I work with AI tools. Sending them links and videos did not stick, because the meaningful change is not learning where a button lives. It is learning a different working rhythm, and the easiest way to understand that rhythm is to do real work beside someone.

Design Pair Sessions is three meetings shaped like pair programming for designers. First it is my turn: I work through a live task while you watch and interrupt whenever you want. Then you drive. The last session focuses on shipping, design judgment, and accessibility.

There are no slides, certificates, or pretend exercises. We use real work and practise the whole messy path from understanding the problem to sharing something another person can use.

## Nerd talk

- Three structured sessions progress from observation to guided practice to independent shipping.
- The curriculum covers research, requirements, flow sketching, incremental building, review, and accessibility.
- The facilitator works on an in-flight task instead of replaying a polished demonstration.
- A self-guided site holds the session structure, prompts, and supporting material.
- The content can evolve as the AI toolkit changes without changing the learning model.
